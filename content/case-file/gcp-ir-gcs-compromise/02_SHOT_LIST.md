## Coverage minimum per episode (para no trabarte en edit)
- 1 cold open card
- 1 screen take clean (no cursor shake)
- 1 screen take with intentional cursor to fields
- 1 outro card
- Optional: 2 hook face takes (A/B take)

## Screen recording specs
- 1920x1080 source then crop to 1080x1920 vertical safe OR record in vertical canvas with log centered
- Font zoom OS 125–150%
- Cursor large
- Dark theme terminal/console
- Hide bookmarks/PII chrome
- Pause between clicks for punch-ins

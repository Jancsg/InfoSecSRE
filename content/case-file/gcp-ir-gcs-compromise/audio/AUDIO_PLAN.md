# AUDIO PLAN

## Chain
DJI Mic 2 TX (solapa, 15–20cm) → RX → iPhone (A-roll) y/o Mac (VO over screen)

## VO performance notes
- Habla como narrador de incidente, no coach motivacional
- Cortes naturales entre frases cortas (ya escritos así)
- Deja 0.3–0.5s de aire entre beats para edits
- Evita “eee”, “o sea”, “hoy vamos a”

## Music bed
- Genre: dark ambient / investigation underscore
- Energy: low, no drums drops
- Level: −24 to −30 dB under VO
- Same track family across all 5 eps (brand continuity)
- Hard stop or fade 0.5s before outro CTA

## SFX (mínimos)
- 1 soft digital tick on cold open (optional)
- 1 muted “mark” when red FAIL box appears (EP03)
- NO whoosh spam

## CapCut audio chain
1. Noise reduction light
2. Voice isolate only if room is bad
3. EQ: high-pass ~80–100Hz
4. Compression gentle
5. Loudness normalize for TikTok dialogue

## Room checklist before record
- AC off if possible
- Fans low
- Phone notifications silent
- 5-second pre-roll silence for NR profile
